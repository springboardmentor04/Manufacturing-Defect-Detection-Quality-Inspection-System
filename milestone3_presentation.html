Module 5 — Defect Classification

Defect Classification &
Severity Scoring Framework

Overall Severity Formula
Severity Score = (Size × 30%) + (Location × 25%)
                      + (Defect Type × 25%) + (Confidence × 20%)
📏 DEFECT SIZE — 30%
Bounding box area relative to image. Large crack = High score.
📍 LOCATION — 25%
Proximity to critical center. Functional area = Higher impact.
🔬 DEFECT TYPE — 25%
Class severity map: Crack 95, Dent 75, Scratch 40.
🤖 CONFIDENCE — 20%
AI model confidence score. Under 70% → Manual review flagged.
Severity Levels
🔴 Critical (80–100) — Reject Product Immediately
🟠 High (60–79) — Repair or Rework Required
🟡 Medium (40–59) — Inspection Review Required
🔵 Low (0–39) — Product Generally Acceptable
📌 Example Result
Surface Crack → Size: 85 · Location: 90 · Type: 95 · Conf: 92
Score: 88 → CRITICAL → Reject Product
